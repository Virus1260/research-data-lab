### Abstract
A conical screw mixer having a downwardly converging conical mixing container and an agitating screw extending adjacent to the container wall. The screw rotates about its own longitudinal axis and simultaneously revolves about the central vertical axis of the container by means of an orbiting swivel arm driven by a central drive shaft. The internal bevel gear transmission within the swivel head is sealed against the container atmosphere by dynamic shaft seals, ensuring reliable transmission of high mixing torque without powder ingress into the gear casing.

---

### Detailed Mechanical Description
The invention relates to a conical screw mixer comprising a conical vessel closed at the top by a lid and tapering toward a bottom discharge opening. An agitating screw runs along the conical vessel wall at a small clearance.

In conventional conical screw blenders, the orbiting swivel arm houses a bevel gear pair that transmits rotation from the stationary sun gear to the screw drive shaft. A recurring failure mode was the ingress of abrasive bulk powder into the swivel arm gearhead, causing rapid tooth wear and grease contamination of the mixing batch.

According to the invention, the drive mechanism is configured such that:
1. The central vertical shaft carries a rotating swivel casing provided with an eccentric bearing seat for the mixing screw shaft.
2. A fixed sun bevel gear is mounted coaxially on the central stationary housing.
3. A planet bevel gear is keyed to the upper end of the mixing screw shaft and meshes with the stationary sun gear.
4. A multi-chamber labyrinth and lip seal assembly is interposed between the rotating screw shaft and the swivel casing, provided with a grease or air purge barrier to hermetically exclude powder dust from the gear housing.
5. The screw flighting has an outer diameter tapering from the top toward the bottom, matching the cone convergence to produce gentle, high-volume upward mass flow with zero dead zones.

### English Translation of Patent Claims (Claims 1 to 8)

1. **Claim 1**: Conical screw mixer comprising a downwardly tapering conical container, a mixing screw extending along the container wall, a central drive shaft driving an orbiting swivel arm, and a transmission arranged within the swivel arm for rotating the mixing screw about its longitudinal axis while revolving it about the central container axis, characterized in that the swivel arm comprises an enclosed gear housing supporting the mixing screw shaft in spaced bearings, wherein a sealing assembly is arranged between the screw shaft and the gear housing comprising an axially loaded dynamic seal and a dust barrier chamber.
2. **Claim 2**: Conical screw mixer according to claim 1, characterized in that the gear housing includes an internal oil bath for splash lubrication of the bevel gears, isolated from the container interior by double radial lip seals.
3. **Claim 3**: Conical screw mixer according to claim 1 or 2, characterized in that the lower end of the mixing screw is free of any bottom guide bearing in the conical container.
4. **Claim 4**: Conical screw mixer according to any one of claims 1 to 3, characterized in that the mixing screw flighting comprises continuous helical stainless steel strip welded to a solid central shaft.
5. **Claim 5**: Conical screw mixer according to claim 4, characterized in that the screw pitch increases progressively from the bottom end toward the top end.
6. **Claim 6**: Conical screw mixer according to any one of claims 1 to 5, characterized in that the container lid is provided with an inspection hatch and sanitary inlet connections.
7. **Claim 7**: Conical screw mixer according to any one of claims 1 to 6, characterized in that the bottom apex of the conical container comprises a sanitary ball valve flush with the container inner contour.
8. **Claim 8**: Conical screw mixer according to any one of claims 1 to 7, characterized in that the swivel arm drive is powered by a hollow-shaft gearmotor mounted centrally on the container cover.
