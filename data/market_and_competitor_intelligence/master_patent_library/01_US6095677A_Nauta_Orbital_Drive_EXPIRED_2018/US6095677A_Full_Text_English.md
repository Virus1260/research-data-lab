# UNITED STATES PATENT US 6,095,677 A
## Drive for a Mixing or Drying Device (Planetary Orbital Swivel Arm Drive)

* **Patent Number**: US 6,095,677 A
* **Grant Date**: August 1, 2000
* **Filing Date**: July 10, 1998 (Priority NL 1006565 July 11, 1997)
* **Inventors**: Jan Hendrik Jansen, Gerrit Hendrik Slot
* **Assignee**: Hosokawa Micron B.V. (Doetinchem, Netherlands)
* **Statutory Expiration Date**: **July 10, 2018 (EXPIRED - 100% PUBLIC DOMAIN)**

### Technical Summary
Discloses the sanitary top-mounted epicyclic planetary gearhead that suspends the mixing screw as a true cantilever inside a downwardly converging conical vessel.
- Sun-and-planet transmission produces dual rotation: self-rotation of the screw along its longitudinal axis (40-75 RPM) and orbital revolution of the screw arm around the vessel wall (1-2.5 RPM).
- Eliminates any bottom bearing, bottom pivot, or bottom seal at the cone apex.
- Allows completely unobstructed bottom product discharge through a flush spherical segment valve.
