# UNITED STATES PATENT US 4,588,301 A
## Conical Screw Mixer Drive Assembly

* **Patent Number**: US 4,588,301 A
* **Grant Date**: May 13, 1986
* **Filing Date**: September 30, 1983 (Priority DE 3245935 December 11, 1982)
* **Inventor**: Alfred Bolz (Wangen im Allgäu, Germany)
* **Assignee**: Alfred Bolz
* **Statutory Expiration Date**: **May 13, 2003 (EXPIRED - 100% PUBLIC DOMAIN)**

### Technical Summary
The US granted equivalent of EP 0 111 067 B1. Establishes the mechanical design of a conical screw mixer with a central drive shaft, an orbiting swivel arm, and bevel gear transmission to drive an Archimedean mixing screw along the inner wall of a conical vessel.
