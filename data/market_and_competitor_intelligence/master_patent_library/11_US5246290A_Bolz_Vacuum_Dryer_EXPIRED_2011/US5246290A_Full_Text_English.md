# UNITED STATES PATENT US 5,246,290 A
## Cone Mixer / Vacuum Contact Dryer with Swivel Arm Drive and Sealing Arrangement

* **Patent Number**: US 5,246,290 A
* **Grant Date**: September 21, 1993
* **Filing Date**: September 16, 1991 (Priority DE 4118592 June 6, 1991)
* **Inventor**: Alfred Bolz (Wangen im Allgäu, Germany)
* **Assignee**: Bolz Alfred GmbH & Co. KG
* **Statutory Expiration Date**: **September 21, 2010 / 2011 (EXPIRED - 100% PUBLIC DOMAIN)**

### Technical Summary
The US granted equivalent of EP 0 517 178 B1. Discloses the sealing and lubrication arrangement for conical vacuum contact dryers (Bolz BS-V series) operating under deep vacuum. Features an external lubricant receptacle connected through the central shaft to mechanical face seals, maintaining a barrier pressure to prevent solvent vapors from migrating into the swivel arm gearhead.
