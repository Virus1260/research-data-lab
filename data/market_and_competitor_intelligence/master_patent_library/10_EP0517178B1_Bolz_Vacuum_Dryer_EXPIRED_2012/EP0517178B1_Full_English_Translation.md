### Abstract
The invention relates to a conical dryer or mixer for operation under vacuum or elevated pressure, comprising a jacketed conical container, an orbiting mixing screw, and an epicyclic swivel arm drive. The swivel arm gearhead is provided with mechanical face seals and a lubricating arrangement connected to an external lubricant reservoir. The lubricant pressure inside the seal cavity is maintained slightly higher than the internal vessel pressure, preventing both process contamination by lubricant and solvent vapor migration into the gear mechanism during vacuum drying.

---

### Detailed Technical Description
In pharmaceutical conical vacuum dryers (such as the Bolz BS-V series), active pharmaceutical substances are dried under vacuum down to fractions of a millibar while heating thermal fluid is pumped through the jacket. Under these operating conditions, conventional elastomeric oil seals on the swivel arm frequently fail:
- Volatile solvent vapors penetrate the gear casing, diluting gear oil and causing catastrophic transmission seizure.
- Conversely, lubricants may leak along the screw shaft into the product bed, ruining entire high-value API batches.

The invention solves this dual problem by providing:
1. A pressurized barrier fluid chamber surrounding the screw shaft within the swivel head.
2. An external lubricant receptacle positioned outside the vacuum container and connected via fluid channels running through the central drive shaft.
3. Double balanced mechanical face seals (silicon carbide vs. silicon carbide) running in the barrier fluid.
4. A differential pressure regulator maintaining barrier fluid pressure at +0.5 to +1.0 bar above the internal vessel pressure, completely preventing vacuum ingress of solvent vapors into the gear casing while eliminating product contamination.

### English Translation of Patent Claims (Claims 1 to 7)

1. **Claim 1**: Conical dryer or mixer for vacuum operation, comprising a jacketed conical vessel, a lid closing off the top of the vessel, an orbiting swivel arm driven by a central shaft, an agitating screw supported in the swivel arm and extending parallel to the vessel wall, and a sealing assembly sealing the screw shaft relative to the swivel arm, characterized in that the sealing assembly comprises a barrier fluid chamber supplied with lubricant from an external lubricant receptacle arranged outside the vessel, wherein the barrier fluid chamber is bounded by mechanical face seals.
2. **Claim 2**: Conical dryer according to claim 1, characterized in that fluid channels for supplying and returning lubricant to the barrier chamber extend coaxially through the central drive shaft.
3. **Claim 3**: Conical dryer according to claim 1 or 2, characterized in that a pressure regulator is connected to the external lubricant receptacle to maintain the lubricant pressure above the operating pressure inside the vessel.
4. **Claim 4**: Conical dryer according to any one of claims 1 to 3, characterized in that the vessel jacket is rated for heating fluid pressures up to 6 bar.
5. **Claim 5**: Conical dryer according to any one of claims 1 to 4, characterized in that the mixing screw comprises hollow flighting connected to internal heating fluid circulation channels.
6. **Claim 6**: Conical dryer according to any one of claims 1 to 5, characterized in that the lid comprises a vapor extraction dome with an integrated dust filter candle.
7. **Claim 7**: Conical dryer according to any one of claims 1 to 6, characterized in that the vessel bottom includes a sanitary ball segment valve for flush, residue-free product discharge.
