   - For Heating (+20°C to +60°C): The same Syltherm XLT passes through an electric circulation heater (15–25 kW with thyristor/SCR control) to provide precisely metered heat of sublimation without scorching the product.

5. HOW DOES THE ACTIVE FREEZE DRYING (AFD) DYNAMIC FREEZING MECHANISM WORK?
   - Unlike conventional shelf freeze dryers that freeze static trays into a solid slab over 6–12 hours, the AFD conical stirred reactor freezes the product dynamically:
