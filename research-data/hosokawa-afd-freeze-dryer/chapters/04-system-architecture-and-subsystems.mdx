## 3. How the Process Cycle Exercises the Subsystems

![Representative Freeze Drying Cycle Profile](/diagrams/freeze_drying_cycle_profile.png)

1. **Aseptic Charging:** API solution or paste is charged via top sanitary port (`N1`) into the purged vessel (pre-inerted with N₂ to $<1\%\text{ O}_2$).
2. **Pre-Cooling & Agitated Granulation:** The TCU chills the vessel jacket to $-40^\circ\text{C}$. The cantilevered auger rotates at low speed (~20–40 rpm), gently tumbling the slurry against the cold wall to prevent bulk freezing into a solid block.
3. **Vacuum Flash Freezing:** The vacuum pump pulls chamber pressure down rapidly. Evaporative surface flash cooling pulls latent heat from within the droplets, fracturing the mass into free-flowing, porous snow granules.
4. **Primary Sublimation:** The vacuum is controlled at $0.05\text{ to }1.5\text{ mbar}$ by throttling valve `PV-102`. The TCU shifts to heating duty ($-10^\circ\text{C}$ to $+20^\circ\text{C}$), delivering $\Delta H_{\text{sub}} = 2,840\text{ kJ/kg}$ into the dynamic bed. Sublimed vapor passes through the +40 °C heated top dome.
5. **Secondary Desorption:** Pressure is lowered to $<0.05\text{ mbar}$ while the jacket is warmed to $+30^\circ\text{C}\text{ to }+40^\circ\text{C}$. Bound moisture desorbs until the Pirani and Capacitance manometer curves converge.
6. **Reverse-Pulse Blowback & Post-Blend:** High-pressure dry N₂ pulses dislodge fines from the filter elements; the auger runs for 5 minutes to homogenize the batch into a single uniform lot.
7. **Sterile Vacuum Release:** The vessel is vented to atmospheric pressure with sterile $0.2\,\mu\text{m}$-filtered nitrogen.
8. **Contained Discharge:** The bottom ball segment valve opens, discharging bulk powder directly into sterile continuous-liner bags or IBC totes.
9. **Automated WIP/CIP & SIP:** The dual-loop CIP skid washes the orbital head (6 bar @ 9 m³/h) and vessel walls (3 bar), followed by 121 °C clean steam sterilization.
