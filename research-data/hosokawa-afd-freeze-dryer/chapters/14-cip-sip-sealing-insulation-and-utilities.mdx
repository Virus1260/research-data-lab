## 1. Clean-In-Place (CIP) Architecture & Spray Nozzle Geometry

Per authentic Hosokawa engineering documentation and CAD standards, CIP decontamination is delivered via a dual-loop fixed-nozzle network:

### A. Cover Spray Nozzles (Drawing 9-4, Doc 8390105_1)

![Hosokawa Drawing 8390105_1: Top Cover Cleaning Nozzle Arrangement](/photos/webinar_captures/nauta_cip_manual_9_4_cover_cleaning_nozzle_arrangement.png)

![Top Cover Cleaning Spray Head Elevation & Shaft Clearance (8390105_1)](/photos/webinar_captures/nauta_cip_manual_9_4_cover_spray_head_elevation.png)

![Sanitary Tri-Clamp Mounting Detail for Cover Spray Lances (8390105_1)](/photos/webinar_captures/nauta_cip_manual_9_4_cover_lance_flange_connection.png)

- **Configuration**: Five (5) fixed sanitary CIP spray nozzles positioned directly in the dished top cover.
- **Pitch & Spacing**: Arranged at a precise **115 mm pitch** around the drive head mounting hub.
- **Coverage Envelope**: Engineered for $360^\circ$ spherical impingement over the orbital arm gearbox, universal joint, cantilevered screw suspension flange, and instrument nozzles.
- **Operating Conditions**:
  - Supply Pressure: **6.0 bar g**
  - Wash Flow Rate: **9.0 m³/hr**
  - Spray Head Hardware: Sanitary fixed spray balls / Toftejorg rotary heads with orbital coverage.

### B. Vessel Wall Spray Nozzles (Drawing 9-5, Doc 8390106_1)

![Hosokawa Drawing 8390106_1: Vessel Wall Wash Cleaning Nozzle Array](/photos/webinar_captures/nauta_cip_manual_9_5_vessel_wall_cleaning_nozzles.png)

![Wall Spray Lance Impingement Trajectory & Fluid Impact Angle (8390106_1)](/photos/webinar_captures/nauta_cip_manual_9_5_wall_spray_lance_trajectory.png)

![Nozzle Elevation & Flow Rate Schedule for Conical Wall Washing (8390106_1)](/photos/webinar_captures/nauta_cip_manual_9_5_wall_nozzle_dimension_schedule.png)

- **Configuration**: Downward-angled wide-angle fan/cone spray nozzles flush-mounted along the upper shell boundary.
- **Coverage Envelope**: Directs high-velocity sheet wetting down the steep conical vessel wall, washing away caked product heels and fines.
- **Operating Conditions**:
  - Supply Pressure: **3.0 bar g**
  - Flow Dynamic: High-volume turbulent cascading film down to the bottom outlet.

### C. Dual WIP/CIP Distribution Manifold (Drawing 9-1, Doc 8390096_1)

![Hosokawa Drawing 8390096_1: Fixed WIP/CIP Dual Spray Lance System](/photos/webinar_captures/nauta_cip_manual_9_1_fixed_wip_cip_dual_nozzle_drawing.png)

![Spray Pattern Trajectory & Shadow-Free Cone Coverage (8390096_1)](/photos/webinar_captures/nauta_cip_manual_9_1_wip_cip_spray_pattern_coverage.png)

- **Supply Loop 1**: Feeds the top cover spray cluster (universal joint and vapor dome).
- **Supply Loop 2**: Feeds the conical wall nozzles and bottom discharge valve cavity.
- **Cycle Progression**:
  1. *Pre-Rinse*: Purified water (ambient, 3–5 min) dislodges bulk powder into the drain.
  2. *Caustic Wash*: 1.5–2.0% NaOH solution heated to 65–80 °C, recirculated for 15–30 min to digest organic API residues.
  3. *Intermediate Rinse*: Purified water rinse until effluent conductivity matches incoming water (< 1.3 µS/cm).
  4. *Acid Wash (optional)*: 0.5–1.0% Phosphoric/Citric acid at 50 °C for mineral scale removal.
  5. *Final WFI Rinse*: Water For Injection (WFI) single-pass rinse to achieve Total Organic Carbon (TOC) < 500 ppb.
  6. *Hot Air / N₂ Blowdown*: Sterile dry air purge drying all internal surfaces prior to vacuum check.

## 2. Sterilize-In-Place (SIP) System

When specified for aseptic biologic manufacturing, the entire enclosed pressure envelope is sterilized with clean saturated steam without autoclaving:

- **Steam Conditions**: Clean/pure steam at **121.1 °C to 123.0 °C** corresponding to **1.1 to 1.3 bar g** saturated steam pressure.
- **Exposure Period**: Validated minimum 30-minute exposure at all cold spots (typically the bottom discharge valve seat and the vapor dome drain line).
- **Thermal Expansion Mitigation**: Conical vessel shell and half-pipe coils expand differentially during steam injection. All guide brackets and vessel support lugs must incorporate slotted sliding pads to absorb axial thermal expansion without pipe-stress cracking.
- **Air Evacuation & Condensate Drainage**: Air is heavier than steam; chamber air must be evacuated via a pre-vacuum cycle (vacuum-assisted SIP) or displaced through a low-point sanitary steam trap assembly with automated temperature logging ($TT-701$).

## 3. Dynamic Sealing & Sanitary Materials

![Orbital Arm Dual Sealing Assemblies: Rotary Mechanical & Lip Seals](/photos/webinar_captures/nauta_orbital_arm_sealing_mechanisms_lip_mechanical.png)


The AFD combines ultra-high vacuum (down to 0.01 mbar) with positive steam pressure (+2.5 bar g) and extreme thermal cycling (−55 °C to +130 °C).

| Seal Location | Seal Technology | Material Specification | Design Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lid Main Closure** | Inflatable or compression O-ring gasket | USP Class VI / FDA EPDM or FFKM (Kalrez) | Tongue-and-groove sanitary clamp retainer preventing gasket extrusion under vacuum |
| **Agitator Top Drive** | Double mechanical seal with sterile gas barrier | Silicon Carbide vs Carbon faces, Hastelloy C-22 springs | Pressurized with sterile N₂ at 1.0 bar *above* vessel pressure; zero oil/grease ingress |
| **Cantilevered Suspension** | Completely eliminates bottom seal | N/A (Top hung only) | Bottom bearing strictly prohibited; prevents submerged seal wear, particulate shedding, and batch contamination |
| **Bottom Discharge Valve** | Spherical ball segment seat | Virgin PTFE / PEEK composite | Seated flush with cone apex; zero dead leg ($L/D = 0$) |
| **Sight Glass & Ports** | Fused borosilicate glass to metal | Borosilicate 3.3 / 316L ferrule | Thermal shock resistant; rated for full vacuum and 4 bar g SIP |

## 4. Vessel Insulation & Cladding

- **Core Insulation**: Non-combustible, chloride-free expanded cellular glass (Pyrogel / Foamglas) or hydrophobic stone wool.
- **Thickness**: 50 mm minimum across the entire cone and top dished head.
- **Outer Protective Cladding**: 1.5 mm thick 304 stainless steel sheet, fully seam-welded and hermetically sealed to prevent washdown ingress during room sanitation.
- **Personnel Protection**: Maintains external surface temperature $< 40\ ^\circ\text{C}$ during 121 °C SIP cycles and $> 10\ ^\circ\text{C}$ during −55 °C cryogenic freezing cycles to prevent condensation sweating and frostbite.

## 5. Utility Management Skid (UMS) Interface Matrix

All plant connections terminate at a centralized stainless-steel utility manifold, isolating factory utilities from the cleanroom perimeter:

| Utility Stream | Design Supply Rating | Machine Usage |
|---|---|---|
| **Clean Electrical Power** | 400/480 VAC, 3-phase, 50/60 Hz | Agitator inverter VFDs, vacuum pump skid, control cabinet |
| **Instrument Air** | 6.0–7.0 bar g, dewpoint −40 °C | Pneumatic valve actuators, pulsejet reverse-blowback |
| **High-Purity Nitrogen (N₂)** | 99.999% purity, 4.0 bar g | Double mechanical seal barrier, filter blowback, vacuum break |
| **Clean/Pure Steam** | 2.5–3.0 bar g, saturated, pyrogen-free | SIP sterilization cycle |
| **Water For Injection (WFI)** | 80 °C, 3.5 bar g, loop circulation | Final CIP rinse |
| **Process Chilled Water** | 6–10 °C supply, 3.0 bar g | Vacuum pump interstage cooling, solvent condenser |
| **TCU Thermal Fluid** | Syltherm XLT / Marlotherm (−65 °C to +150 °C) | Conical jacket heating and cooling |
