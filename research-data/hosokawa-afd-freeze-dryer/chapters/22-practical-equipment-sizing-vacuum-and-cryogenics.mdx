# 22 — Practical Equipment Sizing: Vacuum, -60°C Cryogenics & Safety Interlocks

When transitioning from theoretical academic research to building physical equipment or designing a commercial SolidWorks 3D model, engineers immediately face hard practical questions:

1. *Which physical vacuum pump can realistically sustain 0.04 mbar under continuous ice sublimation without choking or blowing oil into the cleanroom?*
2. *How do we practically achieve -60°C? Can a normal facility chiller do it, or do we need a two-stage cascade refrigeration skid or liquid nitrogen (LN2)?*
3. *What is the exact Cause-and-Effect (C&E) interlock logic that prevents costly equipment destruction or operator hazard?*

This chapter provides the concrete answers, real-world hardware selections, and safety matrices necessary to build a functional active freeze dryer.

---

## 1. Vacuum System Selection: Reaching & Holding 0.04 mbar

In the AFD literature, target operating pressure during primary drying is between **0.02 mbar and 0.10 mbar** (typically **0.04 mbar / 40 µbar / 30 mTorr**).

### Why Conventional Pumps Fail Here:
- **Standard Single-Stage Rotary Vane Pumps**: Stall around 0.5 to 1.0 mbar. They cannot reach 0.04 mbar under continuous gas loads.
- **Oil-Sealed Rotary Pumps**: In a pharmaceutical cleanroom, oil vapor migrates upstream (oil backstreaming) into the sterile chamber, contaminating high-value biopharmaceuticals. Furthermore, water vapor from sublimation emulsifies in the pump oil, turning it into a milky sludge that destroys vacuum performance in hours.

### The Proven Industrial Solution: Dry Screw Pump + Roots Mechanical Booster

For pharmaceutical cGMP production, the industry-standard package is a **two-stage dry vacuum train (`PKG-401`)**:

```
[AFD Vessel V-101] 
       │ 
[Dust Filter V-102] 
       │ 
[Condenser Coil E-101 (-75°C)] ──(99% water vapor caught as ice)──►
       │ 
       ▼ (Non-condensable inert gases + residual trace vapor)
[Roots Blower Booster (e.g., Edwards EH / Leybold RUVAC)]
       │ (10× pressure compression: 0.04 mbar ──► 0.4 mbar)
       ▼
[Multi-Stage Dry Screw Pump (e.g., Edwards GXS / Leybold DRYVAC)]
       │ (Compresion to atmospheric pressure: 0.4 mbar ──► 1013 mbar)
       ▼
[Exhaust to Cleanroom Abatement / Vent]
```

### Commercial Hardware Specifications:
1. **Mechanical Roots Booster (Upstream)**:
   - **Models**: Edwards EH250 / EH500, Leybold RUVAC WA251, or Busch Panda.
   - **Function**: Operates with counter-rotating lobed impellers with zero internal friction. It delivers enormous volumetric pumping displacement (250–500 m³/h) specifically in the 10 mbar to 0.01 mbar range where standard pumps lose throughput.
2. **Backing Dry Screw Pump (Downstream)**:
   - **Models**: Edwards GXS160, Leybold DRYVAC DV450, or Busch Cobra NC 0100.
   - **Function**: Twin synchronized screw rotors that compress gas progressively without requiring sealing oil inside the swept volume (**100% oil-free**).
   - **Ultimate Base Pressure**: Dry screw pumps reach **< 0.005 mbar (5 µbar)** on a blank flange, meaning **0.04 mbar** sits directly in their sweet spot of high pumping efficiency!

## 2. Cryogenic Thermal Engineering: How to Achieve -60°C

A standard facility chilled-water loop (+7°C to +12°C) or single-stage R134a/R410A refrigeration chiller (bottoming out at -20°C to -25°C) **cannot freeze pharmaceutical bulk solutions to their eutectic/glass transition points ($T_g' \approx -35^\circ\text{C}$ to $-50^\circ\text{C}$)**.

To operate the vessel wall at **-45°C to -55°C** and the condenser coil at **-65°C to -75°C**, engineers choose between two proven industrial architectures:

### Option A: Two-Stage Mechanical Cascade Refrigeration Skid (`PKG-301`)
*Best for: Commercial pharma facilities with continuous production and no bulk cryogenic liquid logistics.*

A cascade system connects two independent refrigeration circuits through an intermediate heat exchanger:
- **High-Stage Loop (Warm)**:
  - **Refrigerant**: R404A, R507, or low-GWP R452A.
  - **Evaporator Temperature**: $-30^\circ\text{C}$ to $-35^\circ\text{C}$.
  - **Condenser**: Rejects heat to building cooling-tower water (+25°C) or ambient air.
- **Low-Stage Loop (Cold)**:
  - **Refrigerant**: R23 ($CF_3H$), R508B, or R170 (Ethane).
  - **Condenser**: Chilled by the high stage's evaporator inside a brazed-plate heat exchanger.
  - **Evaporator Temperature**: $-75^\circ\text{C}$ to $-85^\circ\text{C}$.
- **Heat Transfer Fluid (Thermal Oil)**:
  - Water or glycol freezes solid at these temperatures. You must use a specialized synthetic polydimethylsiloxane fluid: **Syltherm XLT** (Dow) or **Marlotherm XC** (Eastman).
  - **Fluid Properties**: Kinematic viscosity remains extraordinarily low (**1.4 cSt at -50°C** and **3.0 cSt at -80°C**), allowing standard centrifugal/magnetic pumps (`TCU-201`) to circulate fluid through the vessel dimple jacket without freezing or causing high pressure drop.

### Option B: Liquid Nitrogen (LN2) Indirect Cryogenic Skid
*Best for: Pilot plants, prototype testing skids, or rapid-freezing bioprocess labs.*

- **Refrigerant**: Liquid Nitrogen delivered from an insulated bulk vacuum tank at $-196^\circ\text{C}$ (77 K) at 3–4 bar.
- **Execution**:
  - LN2 is metered through a cryogenic proportional control valve (e.g. Samson 3241-Cryo or Badger Meter).
  - It flows through a stainless-steel plate-fin heat exchanger that chills the circulating Syltherm XLT loop.
  - The nitrogen boils into cold gas and is safely vented outside the building.
- **Advantages**:
  - Zero mechanical compressors, zero vibration, zero noise, zero fluorinated greenhouse gases.
  - Ultra-rapid chilling: cools from $+20^\circ\text{C}$ to $-60^\circ\text{C}$ in under 12 minutes.

## 3. Cause-and-Effect (C&E) Safety Interlock Matrix

The Safety Instrumented System (SIS) executes dedicated trip logic completely separate from the normal PLC recipe controller. The table below represents the authoritative **Cause-and-Effect Matrix** for the AFD:

| Hazard / Trip Condition (Cause) | Initiating Sensor | System | Interlock Action (Effect) | Safe State Reached |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Vessel Lid Opened Under Vacuum** | `ZS-101` (Limit Switch) | SIS | • Trip Agitator Drive `M-101`<br>• Close Vacuum Valve `PV-102`<br>• Backfill sterile N2 via `XV-104` | Atmospheric pressure restored; operator protected from mechanical nip-points. |
| **Emergency Stop Actuated** | `ESD-001` (E-Stop Button) | Hardwired Relay | • Drop 3-phase contactor `KM-01`<br>• Cut power to VFD, heaters & pumps<br>• Spring-close all pneumatic isolation valves | System immediately unpowered and pneumatically locked. |
| **Agitator Mechanical Over-Torque** | `JIT-101` (>150% FLA for 3s) | BPCS & SIS | • Stop Agitator Motor `M-101`<br>• Alarm operator<br>• Hold temperature loop | Prevents mechanical shearing of orbital screw or gearbox damage. |
| **Condenser High Temperature** | `TT-103` (> -40°C during drying) | BPCS Interlock | • Inhibit jacket heaters (`FCV-201` closed)<br>• Ramp jacket fluid to -10°C<br>• Alert operator | Prevents bulk ice melt-back and product boil-over in the vessel. |
| **Chamber Overpressure** | `PSH-101` (> 1.2 bar absolute) | SIS & Mechanical | • Trip clean steam feed `PV-502`<br>• Mechanical safety relief `PSV-501` lifts | Prevents rupture of conical vessel or sight glasses during SIP. |
| **Cooling Fluid Circulation Failure** | `FS-201` (Flow Switch Low) | BPCS Interlock | • De-energize jacket electric heaters<br>• Protect circulation pump from dry run | Prevents thermal oil breakdown or heater element burnout. |

## 4. Chronological Step-by-Step Cycle Execution

For engineers commissioning an AFD or running their initial trial batch, the table below defines the exact operational sequence:

| Step # | Operation Name | Vessel Target (°C) | Pressure Target (mbar) | Agitator (RPM) | Active Subsystems |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Sterile Nitrogen Inerting** | +20°C | 1000 mbar | 0 | Chamber purged with sterile N2 until $O_2 < 1\%$. |
| **2** | **Pre-Chilling** | -30°C | 1000 mbar | 0 | `PKG-301` active; Syltherm XLT chills conical vessel jacket. |
| **3** | **Liquid Feed Charge** | -30°C to -10°C | 1000 mbar | 15–20 | Liquid bulk fed through aseptic inlet valve `XV-104`. |
| **4** | **Dynamic Freezing** | -50°C | 1000 mbar | 25–40 | Continuous stirring freezes solution into granular frozen ice beads. |
| **5** | **Deep Evacuation** | -50°C | Pull down to 0.04 mbar | 10–15 | Condenser `E-101` at -75°C. Roots booster engages below 10 mbar. |
| **6** | **Primary Sublimation** | Ramp -50°C ➔ -15°C | 0.04 to 0.08 mbar | 10–20 | `TIC-201` provides sublimation heat; `PIT-101A/B` monitor vapor. |
| **7** | **Secondary Desorption** | Ramp -15°C ➔ +25°C | 0.02 to 0.04 mbar | 15–25 | Bound moisture desorbed; target residual moisture < 1.0%. |
| **8** | **N2 Backfill & Discharge** | +20°C | 950 mbar | 5–10 | Dry N2 backfills chamber. Bottom ball-segment `XV-105` discharges loose powder. |
